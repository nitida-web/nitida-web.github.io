The decision path

Students do not enroll in a single click.

They move through a sequence of questions. The assessment identifies where clarity, confidence, or momentum breaks down.

01

Understand

Can a first-time visitor explain the program, audience, outcome, format, and next step?

02

Trust

03

Enroll

What I review

Six lenses. One enrollment goal.

Every observation is tied to a user question, likely impact, and concrete recommendation. No generic list of visual preferences.

01

Offer clarity

Audience, outcome, program structure, learning format, and value.

02

Program choice

How visitors compare options and identify the right path for their goal or level.

03

Trust placement

Instructor credibility, student outcomes, FAQs, and risk reduction near decisions.

04

Navigation

Whether key pages, actions, and information match the visitor's mental model.

05

06

Enrollment steps

What happens before, during, and immediately after application or checkout.
